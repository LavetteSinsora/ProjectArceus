Why the Intrinsic Curiosity Module (ICM) fails to explore LS20 Level 2

exp_011 · faithful Pathak-2017 ICM + CNN/PPO · real LS20 · terminal-only reward · 3 seeds/level · evidence from saved metrics & checkpoints (seed0 for checkpoint probes).
ICM fails on L2 not because the forward model becomes perfect, but because the auto-calibrated curiosity gain η is frozen against the huge UNTRAINED forward error; once the model learns (error drops ~60×), r^i is permanently ~60–100× smaller than the PPO entropy bonus, so a weak-but-still-present novelty signal can never restructure a policy that keeps dying at a fixed ~66-step depth. The novelty contrast survives — it is just too small to matter.

1 · The collapse is real but identical on the level ICM SOLVED — so collapse alone is not the cause

2 · There IS a novelty contrast on L2 — the model is not perfect, and it is not flat

3 · The mechanism: η is frozen against the untrained error, so r^i lives below the PPO floor

4 · Behavioural ground truth: the policy is trapped at a fixed depth and barely out-explores random

Precise mechanism. (1) ICM's r^i = (η/2)‖φ̂(s')−φ(s')‖²; η is auto-set on the first rollout against the untrained forward error (~23), then frozen (η=2.98e-4). (2) The forward model learns within a couple of updates, dropping the error ~60×, which mechanically drops r^i ~60× to ~6e-5 — far below the PPO entropy bonus (0.01) and the +1 goal reward. (3) A weak per-step novelty bonus that still has contrast (first-seen 1.2–2.5× revisited) but is two orders of magnitude too small cannot generate enough advantage to drive the policy past the ~66-step death point of a ~60-action puzzle, so coverage plateaus at ≤44 states and the agent never sees its first reward. This refines the project's prior note: the culprit is η-renormalisation crushing the magnitude, not a literally perfect model and not a literal loss of contrast.

Implication. What would work on L2 is exploration whose drive does not decay with the learner's own accuracy and is scaled to the task: either re-normalising/annealing η to a fixed fraction of the policy objective, or count/coverage-based novelty (episodic, e.g. RND or Go-Explore-style state archiving) that rewards reaching new depth rather than transient prediction error — consistent with the prior project finding that only Go-Explore cracked L2.
